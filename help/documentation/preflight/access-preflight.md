# Access Preflight

Preflight is available in the AEM Sites authoring environments, including Universal Editor, Document-based authoring, and AEM Sites Page Editor. Preflight opens as a panel on the page you are authoring. From the panel, you [run audits](./audits.md) on that page and Preflight [displays any opportunities](./audit-results.md) it finds to enhance the page before publishing.

* **Universal Editor** – The Preflight Extension appears in the **side rail**. Select it to open the Preflight panel.
* **Document-based authoring** – Open the Preflight panel from the Sidekick or a bookmarklet on your previewed page content.
* **AEM Sites Page Editor** – On AEM 2026.7.0 (release 27083) or later, select the **Preflight** icon in the editor toolbar to open the Preflight panel. On earlier releases, use the Preflight bookmarklet in your browser instead.

After the panel opens, select **Analyze page** to start an audit of the current page.

For setup instructions, see [Preflight setup](./setup.md).
