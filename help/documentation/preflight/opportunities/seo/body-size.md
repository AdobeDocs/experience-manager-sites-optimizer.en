# Body size audit

The **Body size** audit reviews the amount of body content on your page. Pages with very little content can be less useful to readers and may rank poorly in search results. The audit flags pages that appear to have insufficient content.

When the audit finds opportunities, each one describes the problem and the recommended change. To learn how to review and resolve opportunities, see [Audit results in Preflight](../../audit-results.md).
