# Readability audit

The **Readability** audit reviews how easy your page content is to read. Clear, well-structured content keeps readers engaged and helps a wider audience understand your message. The audit evaluates the content and provides actionable recommendations for improvement.

The Readability audit supports content in multiple languages, including:

* English
* French
* Spanish
* Italian
* German
* Dutch

When the audit finds opportunities, each one describes the problem and the recommended change. To learn how to review and resolve opportunities, see [Audit results in Preflight](../../audit-results.md).
