# Headings audit

The **Headings** audit reviews the heading structure of your page. A clear, ordered heading hierarchy helps both readers and search engines understand how your content is organized. The audit flags problems such as headings that skip levels or appear out of order.

When the audit finds opportunities, each one identifies the affected heading and describes the recommended change. To learn how to review and resolve opportunities, see [Audit results in Preflight](../../audit-results.md).
