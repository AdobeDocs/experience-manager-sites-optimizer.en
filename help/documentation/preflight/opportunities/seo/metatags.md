# Metatags audit

The **Metatags** audit reviews the metadata tags on your page, such as the page title and meta description. Well-formed metadata helps search engines understand your content and improves how your page appears in search results. The audit flags tags that are missing, duplicated, or outside the recommended length.

When the audit finds opportunities, each one identifies the affected tag and its current value, and may include an AI-generated suggestion for an improved value. To learn how to review and resolve opportunities, see [Audit results in Preflight](../../audit-results.md).
